CHAPTER 1: 1. Introduction
SOURCE KNOWLEDGE
Teofilo Kisanji University, commonly known as TEKU, is a
university located in Mbeya, Tanzania. The university provides higher
education and training to students in a variety of academic and
professional fields. TEKU is associated with the Moravian Church in
Tanzania and has developed into an institution serving students from
different parts of Tanzania and beyond. The university provides an
environment for academic learning, professional development,
research, innovation, and community service.
This document is designed to provide the knowledge required by a
chatbot that assists students, applicants, parents, visitors, staff,
and members of the public who want information about Teofilo Kisanji
University. The chatbot should use this information to answer
questions about the university, its academic activities, admissions,
student life, facilities, and general services.
Source note: This chapter is derived from the user's supplied TEKU chatbot knowledge documentation. Where the document itself warns that a fact
is time-sensitive, the chatbot should verify it against the latest official TEKU publication.

CHAPTER 2: 2. University Name and Identity
SOURCE KNOWLEDGE
The official name of the institution is Teofilo Kisanji
University. The commonly used abbreviation is TEKU. When users refer
to "Teofilo Kisanji University," "TEKU," "Teku
University," or "the University of TEKU," they are
generally referring to the same institution in Mbeya, Tanzania.
TEKU is a higher-learning institution based in Mbeya. The
university's activities include teaching, learning, research,
consultancy, innovation, and community engagement. The university
serves students pursuing different levels of higher education and
professional development.

CHAPTER 3: 3. Location
SOURCE KNOWLEDGE
Teofilo Kisanji University is located in Mbeya, Tanzania. Mbeya is
a major city in the Southern Highlands of Tanzania and is an
important centre for education, business, transportation, and other
economic activities. The university's location makes it accessible to
students from Mbeya and other regions of Tanzania.
When a user asks where TEKU is located, the chatbot should explain
that the university is in Mbeya, Tanzania. If the user asks for exact
directions, the chatbot should provide the official location
information available from the university or recommend using an
official university contact or a current map service because routes
and landmarks can change.

CHAPTER 4: 4. History and Background
SOURCE KNOWLEDGE
Teofilo Kisanji University was established as an institution of
higher learning associated with the Moravian Church in Tanzania. The
university developed from the educational activities of the church
and was established to provide higher education and contribute to the
social, economic, moral, and professional development of Tanzanian
society.
The university is named after Teofilo Kisanji, an important figure
in the history of the Moravian Church and education in Tanzania. The
institution continues to emphasize education, character development,
service, and academic development.

CHAPTER 10: 10. Academic Programs
SOURCE KNOWLEDGE
TEKU offers academic and professional programs in different
fields. Areas associated with the university include education,
business and economics, social sciences, law, computer and
information-related studies, environmental studies, theology, and
other disciplines.
The exact list of undergraduate, postgraduate, diploma,
certificate, and other programs may change from one academic year to
another. Therefore, the chatbot should treat the current university
prospectus and official admissions announcements as the primary
source when a user asks for the current list of programs.
If a user asks, "Does TEKU offer Computer Science?" the
chatbot should verify the current program list before giving a
definitive answer. If the information is available in the chatbot's
current database, it should provide the program name, level, entry
requirements, duration, and other relevant information.

CHAPTER 11: 11. Undergraduate Studies
SOURCE KNOWLEDGE
Undergraduate education at TEKU is designed to provide students
with foundational and advanced knowledge in their chosen fields.
Students may pursue bachelor's degrees and other undergraduate
qualifications depending on the programs available during a
particular academic year.
Admission into undergraduate programs depends on the
qualifications required by the university and relevant Tanzanian
higher-education regulations. Applicants should provide the required
academic certificates and complete the appropriate application
procedure.
The chatbot should not create admission requirements when they are
not available. If the user asks for exact grades or subject
combinations, the chatbot should provide the current official
requirements for the specific program and academic year.

CHAPTER 14: 14. Admission
SOURCE KNOWLEDGE
Admission is the process through which qualified applicants apply
to study at TEKU. Applicants should select an appropriate program,
confirm that they meet the entry requirements, submit the required
information and documents, and follow the university's application

APPENDIX B — ANSWER GENERATION POLICY
• Answer from retrieved TEKU evidence first. Gemini should be the language generator, not the authority on TEKU facts.
• When evidence is missing, say that the information is not available in the current knowledge base.
• For fees, deadlines, programme availability, admission requirements and contact details, prefer current official sources.
• When two official current sources disagree, state the discrepancy and recommend the latest programme-specific
instruction or direct verification with the responsible office.
• Never expose private student records or ask for passwords, PINs, OTPs or confidential credentials.
